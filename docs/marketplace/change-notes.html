1.2.7: Several selected ranges and Format cells.

  • Select several ranges as in Excel: Ctrl+click or Ctrl+drag (Cmd on a Mac) on cells, row headers or column headers adds them to the selection. Formatting, Format cells…, clearing, hiding, row heights and column widths, merging, fill down and right, and Ctrl+Enter act on all of them, and the status bar totals them. Enter and Tab move through them.
  • Ranges over the same rows, or over the same columns, are copied together and pasted as one block, so two columns far apart paste side by side. One copied cell pastes into all selected ranges. Cut, inserting and deleting rows and columns, sorting, filters and AutoSum work on one range at a time.
  • Format cells… in the cell context menu and at the top of the Format menu sets the number format, alignment, font, borders and fill in one dialog. Only the settings you change are applied.
  • The cell context menu shows an icon for each command, also for the kinds of Paste special and in the Insert and Delete submenus. The same commands in the Edit, Format and Data menus show the same icons.
  • More decimals and Fewer decimals go up to 15 decimal places, as Format cells… does (10 before).
  • Fixed: after copying a range with hidden rows, pasted formulas pointed at the wrong rows, Paste special > Values only read the wrong cells, and a cut cleared a hidden row instead of the last copied row.

1.2.6: Security fixes.

  • The server checks every change and every stored spreadsheet strictly: cell styles, tables, conditional formatting, data validation, charts, Solver models and scenarios. Spreadsheets saved with earlier versions are checked the same way.
  • The spreadsheet shows stored values only after checking them again.
  • Limits keep very large or very slow changes, pastes and imports from slowing down the issue for other people.
  • A formula that needs more calculation than the spreadsheet does at once shows #CALC! (its tooltip says why) instead of freezing the page, and so do formulas that use its result. Sorting, filters, copying, removing duplicates and downloads first wait for the values they need.
  • REGEXTEST, REGEXEXTRACT and REGEXREPLACE can no longer freeze the page. Back references and lookarounds in their patterns are not supported (they give #VALUE!).
  • The guest account can no longer edit when Everyone who can see the issue can edit is on, and anonymous visitors no longer see who else has the spreadsheet open.
  • In CSV downloads, text that starts like a formula (=, +, -, @) gets a leading ', so other spreadsheet apps do not run it.
  • Fixed: colors picked for a new color scale were not kept.
  • Fixed: next to a merged cell that continues below the frozen rows, or whose first rows are hidden, cells were drawn under the wrong column; such merged cells stretched the frozen rows; and the text of a very tall merged cell was drawn too low.

1.2.3: A new name, formula separators and CSV options.

  • The app is now called Spreadsheets for YouTrack and has an updated logo.
  • Formulas follow your language: where decimals are written with a comma (1,5), formulas use ; between arguments and , in numbers, for example =ROUND(1,5; 0). Other languages keep , and . (=ROUND(1.5, 0)).
  • View > Formula separators: Automatic, Comma or Semicolon, for you in this browser. Formulas are stored in one form, so people with different settings share them.
  • Download sheet as CSV asks for the separator between values (comma, semicolon or tab) and the decimal separator of numbers, and remembers your choice.
  • Formula separators are also in the bar's ⋯ menu.
  • Buttons that open a menu or a color picker (the bar's ⋯, More tools, number format, borders, merge, alignment, AutoSum, text and fill color, all sheets) close it on a second click.
  • Fixed: formulas typed with semicolons were written to .xlsx files with semicolons, which other spreadsheet apps cannot read.

1.1.0: What-if analysis.

  • Data > Solver…: maximize, minimize or reach a value of an objective cell by changing variable cells, subject to ≤, =, ≥, integer, binary and AllDifferent constraints.
  • Three solving methods with their options: Simplex LP (with branch and bound for integer constraints), GRG Nonlinear (forward or central derivatives, Multistart) and Evolutionary (population size, mutation rate, random seed, time without improvement).
  • Show Iteration Results, Stop, and a prompt to continue when the time or iteration limit is reached.
  • Solver Results: keep the solution or restore the original values, save it as a scenario, and return to the Solver Parameters dialog.
  • Answer, Sensitivity (shadow prices, reduced costs and allowable ranges, or Lagrange multipliers and reduced gradients), Limits, Population, Feasibility, Feasibility-Bounds and Linearity reports, added as new sheets.
  • Solver models are saved with each sheet and follow inserted and deleted rows and columns; Load/Save stores a model in cells.
  • Data > What-if analysis: Goal Seek, and the Scenario Manager with Add, Edit, Delete, Show, Merge and Summary.
  • Solver models and scenarios are written to .xlsx downloads and read from .xlsx imports.
